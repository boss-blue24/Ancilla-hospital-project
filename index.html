About Us

Established in 1997 by the Congregation of the Handmaids of the Holy Child Jesus (HHCJ), Ancilla Catholic Hospital was founded to address the critical need for accessible, ethical, and high-quality healthcare for the common man in Ifako, Iju, and surrounding areas of Lagos. Our foundation is built on the core Catholic values of dignity, compassion, and service to humanity. We operate as a multispecialist center, constantly striving for clinical excellence while ensuring our services remain affordable and reach the most underserved communities. We are committed not just to treatment, but to comprehensive well-being and preventive health.

Our Mission

To provide standard and affordable health services that are based on holistic approach and that affirm human dignity and respect for life. A healthcare delivery system which effectively complements governmental efforts at all levels by well-articulated and highly motivated professional team thereby impacting positively on the community with significant reduction in maternal and child mortality and morbidity, blindness and disability.

Our Vision

Continuation of the compassion and healing ministry of Christ in relief of human suffering.

Our Medical Services

We provide a full range of medical services from consultation to diagnosis and treatment.

General Surgery & ICU

Expert care in sugery, including abdominal and trauma procedures, supported by a dedicated 24/7 intensive Care Unit (ICU).

Obstetrics & Gynacology

Comprehensive maternal and reproductive health services, including antenatal care, safe delivery, and gynecological treatments.

Dental Clinic

Dental care, including root canal treatment, teeth whitening, veneers & Braces

Family & Internal Medicine

Personalized, continuous care for individuals and families, focusing on chronic disease management and preventive health check-ups.

Pediatrics & Child Health

Dedicated services for infants, children, and adolescents, including immunization, developmental screening, and specialized illness management.

Diagnosis & Laboratory

State-of-the-art laboratory services, medical imaging (Ultrasound, X-ray), and advanced diagnostics for accurate and rapid results.
